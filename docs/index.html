Designed for Drafting & Revision

A workspace built around the day-to-day process of writing and assembling longer manuscripts.

📜

The 55 Scenic Maxims

Brief craft reminders on pacing, physical detail, and scene construction synthesized from Henry James, Caroline Gordon, and Flannery O'Connor, available right in your side drawer.

⚡

5-Minute Typing Warm-Up

A rotating selection of short masterwork excerpts from writers like G.K. Chesterton, Willa Cather, and Herman Melville to type through as a quick warm-up before you begin drafting.

✒️

Draft Metrics & Revision

Non-destructive feedback while revising: check word counts, inspect readability estimates, highlight potential passive phrasing or adverbs, and look up definitions via Wiktionary.

🗂️

Portfolios & Sheets

Group chapters into folder portfolios and write individual scenes on movable cards. Rename any card directly on the board with a double-click.

⛶

Full-Screen Writing

Press F11 or Ctrl+Shift+F to hide toolbars and distracting chrome so you can focus on the current page.

📄

Word & Manuscript Export

Compile chapters into a single manuscript or export individual scenes directly to standard Microsoft Word (.docx) documents with standard margins and serif formatting.

📦

Archive Drawer

Move completed drafts, cut scenes, and alternate outlines out of view without permanently deleting them. Review and restore them whenever needed.

🛡️

Local Auto-Save & Backups

Your work is saved automatically to local JSON files on your disk, with automatic backup (.bak) rotation to safeguard against power loss or crashes.

Installation Instructions

Choose your operating system below for simple installation steps.

Windows Installation (10 / 11)

  1. Download the installer: Morada-Setup-<version>.exe.
  2. Double-click the installer. If Windows SmartScreen appears on unverified binaries, click More info and then Run anyway.
  3. Follow the setup wizard to create desktop and start menu shortcuts.

macOS Installation (Apple Silicon & Intel)

  1. Download the disk image: Morada-<version>.dmg.
  2. Open the downloaded DMG file and drag Morada into your Applications folder.
  3. If macOS Gatekeeper asks for verification on first open, right-click (or Control-click) Morada in Applications and choose Open.

Linux Installation

  1. AppImage (Any Distro): Download Morada-<version>.AppImage, grant execute permission, and launch: chmod +x Morada-*.AppImage && ./Morada-*.AppImage
  2. Debian / Ubuntu: Download morada_<version>_amd64.deb and install via terminal: sudo dpkg -i morada_*_amd64.deb
